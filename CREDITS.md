## Using this theme

The example content (company, people, addresses, posts) is made up. A site built from this theme does not have to keep the credit line in the footer; see `src/config/credit.mjs`. If you keep it, thank you.

Spotted a missing credit or a wrong license? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/nviewsweb/astrowebtheme-free/issues/new/choose).
