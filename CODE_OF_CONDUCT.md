# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or first language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are learning.
- Use clear, plain language. English is not everyone's first language; short sentences help.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you are unsure.
- Respect other people's time: search first, give details, say thank you.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual or violent language and images.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project and repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, open a private report from the **Security** tab (choose **Report a vulnerability**) and write "Conduct report" in the title, or tag a maintainer in the thread. Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions.
